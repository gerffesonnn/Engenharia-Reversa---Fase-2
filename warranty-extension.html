LAMBORGHINI AFTER-SALES

DRIVE WORRY-FREE WITH OUR THREE-YEAR WARRANTY

With us, you get more than the highest performance; you also get the guarantee of reliability and protection that allows for a supreme, worry-free driving experience.

On all new vehicles — starting from the date of delivery and under normal conditions of use — Lamborghini provides a three-year, unlimited-mileage warranty.*

This means guaranteed peace of mind for you from 0 to 3 years and 0 to infinite mileage. But it doesn’t have to end there because you can keep it for longer thanks to our Warranty Extension program. Or you can secure it through our Selezione program.

*Warranty conditions may vary by country. Official Lamborghini Dealers and Service Points are able to provide detailed information on the terms and conditions of the warranty coverage provided for your vehicle.

LAMBORGHINI SELEZIONE

“EXTENDED PROTECTION” FOR THE MAXIMUM PROTECTION

The Warranty Extension covers parts and labour included in the Selezione program, with Original Spare Parts and work performed by Lamborghini specialists.

For the Selezione program, the car must be no more than 10 years old and have no more than 70,000 km (100,000 km for Urus). To renew the warranty, the car must pass the 150 tests of the Lamborghini checklist.

For information on the characteristics and duration of the specific options for each car, please contact an official Lamborghini Dealer or Service Point. Terms and conditions may vary depending on the specific market. The service may be provided by an insurance provider.
