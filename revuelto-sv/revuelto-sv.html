OVERVIEW

THE QUANTUM LEAP IN DRIVING EXPERIENCE

The Revuelto SV represents a revolutionary step in Lamborghini's history. Combining a naturally aspirated 6.5-liter V12 engine with three high-performance electric motors, it delivers an unmatched output of 1065 CV. Lightweight carbon fiber monocoque design and GT3-derived aerodynamics establish a new benchmark for super sports cars.

DESIGN & AERODYNAMICS

SHAPED BY THE WIND

Every line of the Revuelto SV is sculpted for ultimate aerodynamic efficiency. Featuring a double-wing profile fixed rear spoiler and Y-shaped LED architecture, the body creates 80% more downforce compared to the standard model while providing optimal cooling to the high-revving hybrid engine.

POWERTRAIN

HEART OF A MONSTER

At the core lies the legendary mid-mounted V12 engine paired with an 8-speed dual-clutch transmission and dual front axial-flux electric motors. This architecture provides dynamic torque vectoring and instantaneous acceleration out of any corner.
