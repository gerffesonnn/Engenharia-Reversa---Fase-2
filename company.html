COMPANY PROFILE

MADE IN SANT’AGATA BOLOGNESE

Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, the company has spent more than six decades shaping the super sports car through distinctive design and bold engineering. Its home remains in Sant’Agata Bolognese, Italy.

FROM 1963 TO TODAY

LANDMARKS

FULL HISTORY ↗
  1. 1963

    A NEW COMPANY TAKES SHAPE

    Ferruccio Lamborghini establishes his automotive company in Emilia-Romagna.

  2. 1966

    MIURA CHANGES THE FORMULA

    A mid-engine layout helps define a new generation of road-going supercars.

  3. 1974

    COUNTACH ARRIVES

    A radical wedge-shaped design becomes one of the marque’s defining silhouettes.

  4. 1986

    THE LM002 GOES OFF-ROAD

    Lamborghini takes its V12 engineering into an entirely different terrain.

  5. 1990

    DIABLO SETS A NEW PACE

    A new flagship carries the brand into a new decade of performance.

  6. 2001

    A NEW CHAPTER WITH AUDI

    Lamborghini joins the Audi Group and begins a new phase of development.

  7. 2011

    AVENTADOR V12 EVOLUTION

    A new flagship advances Lamborghini’s V12 tradition.

  8. 2018

    URUS OPENS A NEW SEGMENT

    The first Super SUV brings Lamborghini performance to a broader format.

  9. 2024

    A NEW ERA OF ELECTRIFICATION

    Urus SE and Temerario mark another step in the brand’s evolving lineup.

No milestones are listed for this decade.
